A Letter From Your Son

Dear Dad,

Happy 58th Birthday! ❤️

Today, I am grateful to God for the gift of having you as my father. Thank you for your sacrifices, your guidance, your wisdom, and the countless ways you have continued to provide for and support our family.

I may not always express it, but I recognise and appreciate everything you do. Your strength, hard work, and dedication inspire me to become a better person and someone you can always be proud of.

I hope that one day, I will be able to give back even a fraction of everything you have given me. I want to become a man whose achievements bring you joy and whose character makes you proud to call him your son.

Thank you for being my father. Thank you for everything, including the things I may never fully understand or be able to repay.

I love you, Dad. Happy Birthday!

With love, Your Son ❤️
